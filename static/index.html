What is reverse.watch?

A shared, open record of trade reversals across the Steam ecosystem. When a marketplace, trading tool, or community platform reverses a trade due to fraud, scams, or policy violations, that report can be contributed to the database. Anyone can then look up a Steam ID and check whether it has appeared in past reversals, regardless of which platform originally flagged it.

For faster access while browsing, reverse.watch is also integrated into our extension.

Want to contribute?

If you run a marketplace, trading tool, or community site and want to participate by contributing reversal reports, reach out at join@reverse.watch.

The project is also open source, so if you'd like to help with issues or contribute code, you can find the repository on GitHub.
